Everything you need to know about compatibility, activation, and performance.

MotionKit Pro & Wheel are compatible with Adobe After Effects 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025+, as well as Premiere Pro 2022+. It runs natively on both Windows 10/11 and macOS (including native Apple Silicon M1, M2, M3, and Intel chips).
It is strictly a one-time payment. You pay once ($3.00 / ₹249) and own the license forever with all future v1.x feature releases included. There are zero recurring monthly or annual charges.
The wheel is activated via a customizable hotkey (or mouse gesture). A radial menu appears around your cursor with 9 directional positions. Simply flick in that direction to instantly trigger the action without ever looking at a menu bar.
The AI engine uses an optimized local instance of OpenAI's Whisper model that runs directly on your computer's CPU/GPU. No audio ever leaves your machine, no internet connection is required during transcription, and you never have to pay third-party API tokens.
Each standard license covers up to 2 computers simultaneously (for example, your studio workstation and your travel laptop).
Your license key appears immediately on screen right after checkout, and a confirmation email containing your key and download links is automatically dispatched to your inbox.
